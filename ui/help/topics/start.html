Start here

Clipjump keeps everything you copy. Each copy goes on top of a stack, and paste mode walks back through that stack instead of pasting only the newest clip.

Paste mode

Hold Ctrl and press V. Keep holding Ctrl: each further press of V steps one clip further back, and C steps forward again. The card near your pointer shows which clip you are on. Release Ctrl to paste it.

Channels

Clips live in channels, which are separate stacks. Up and Down in paste mode switch channels, and the Channel Organizer moves clips between them.

The rings

Two things open a ring at your pointer: Z in paste mode opens the format ring, and Action Mode opens the action ring. Up to eight wedges sit around a hub that names whichever wedge you point at. When there are more entries than that, the eighth wedge is More and turns the page.

Flick out to a wedge and release the key you are holding, click a wedge, or press its letter; Esc closes the ring.
